# Paper check-in with Prof. Hartley

Fri Oct 16, 11:00 to 11:30, Prof. Hartley's office

## Notes

### Thesis
- Hartley isn't convinced by turnover and seasonality. Clubs and Facebook survive turnover fine, so it can't be the main reason.
- Theo's sharper version: campus marketplaces never get liquid in the first place. They stall at zero.
- She liked "stall at zero" but wants it narrowed to campus marketplaces specifically, not platforms in general.
- Key question she pushed on: what's the relevant network? Theo said a residential college. She said "then show me that, don't assert it."

### Multi-homing
- Theo's claim that GroupMe and Facebook groups keep new marketplaces from tipping is interesting but currently vibes.
- She suggested the ride-hailing driver literature as an analogy, since drivers run several apps at once.
- Better still, collect a little campus data. Count how many listings show up in more than one place.

### Case study
- She suggested using Swapshelf's pilot as the case in section 4. Two colleges in the pilot against the rest of Yale is close to a natural comparison.
- Needs the waitlist split by college and whatever listing data exists by Nov 10.
- Be upfront in the paper that Theo is a co-founder.

### Theory
- Reread Rochet and Tirole on which side gets subsidised. On a campus it's probably the lister.
- She'll send Armstrong 2006 for the competitive bottleneck idea.
