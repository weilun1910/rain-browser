# Swapshelf kickoff

Fri Oct 2, 9:00 to 10:00pm, Branford common room

## Notes

### Roles
- Sam: design, onboarding and the listing flow in SwiftUI
- Theo: backend on Supabase, the browse and request flow, and the paper-side research
- Both: user interviews and recruiting

### What we're building first
- Listings for textbooks and dorm gear
- Requests with a hold so things don't get double-claimed
- Credits instead of money
- Only visible inside your residential college

### Where to start
- Branford, since we both live there and know people
- Saybrook as the second college, because it shares the old Memorial Quadrangle with Branford and the walk is nothing
- Not all of Yale. Sam wanted to go big. Theo argued an empty app in fourteen colleges is worse than a busy one in two. Agreed to start with two.

### Stack
- SwiftUI, iOS 18 and up
- Supabase for auth, Postgres and storage
- Yale email magic links for sign in

### Before writing code
- Nine or ten interviews first, mostly Branford and Saybrook
- A waitlist page to see if anyone cares
