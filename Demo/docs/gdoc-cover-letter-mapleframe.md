# Cover letter - Mapleframe APM intern

Dear Mapleframe team,

I'm applying for the APM internship for summer 2027. Omar Haddad on the comments team suggested I apply after we talked at the Yale CS career fair.

I use Mapleframe almost every day with my co-founder Sam. We're building Swapshelf, an app for swapping textbooks inside Yale's residential colleges, and every flow in the app started as a Mapleframe board. The comment threads on those boards are where most of our product decisions happened, including the biggest one so far: replacing in-person handoffs with pickup shelves after nine user interviews.

What draws me to Mapleframe is how much of the product is about people working together in real time. Cursors, presence and comments change how a small team argues and decides. I've felt that firsthand, and I'd like to work on making it better for teams bigger than two.

I study Computer Science and Economics at Yale. I can build what I design in SwiftUI and Postgres, and I'm writing a seminar paper on why two-sided marketplaces stall at launch, which has made me careful about the difference between users signing up and users getting value.

I'd love to bring that to the APM program. Thank you for considering me.

Best,
Theo Park
