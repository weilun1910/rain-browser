# Swapshelf App Store listing

Draft for the TestFlight description now and the real listing in January.

## Name
Swapshelf

## Subtitle (30 characters max)
Swap books inside your college

## Promotional text
Give the textbooks you're done with. Take the ones you need. Everything stays inside your residential college.

## Description
Swapshelf is the easiest way to pass things on to people who live right next to you.

List a textbook, a lamp or a mini fridge in under a minute. Scan the ISBN and the details fill themselves in. When someone in your college wants it, drop it on the shelf in your buttery with its pickup code. That's it. No meetups, no flaking, no Venmo.

Every item you give earns you a credit. Every item you take costs one. New members start with one credit, so you can take something on your first day.

- Only students in your residential college can see your listings
- Sign in with your Yale email
- Report or block anyone, any time

Swapshelf is a student project. It's free, and it'll stay free.
