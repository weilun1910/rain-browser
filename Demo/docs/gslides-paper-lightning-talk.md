# Lightning talk - Stuck at zero

Five minutes in week 11, Nov 11. Five slides, one minute each.

## Slide 1 - Stuck at zero
Why campus marketplaces stall, and why it happens at the start rather than later.

## Slide 2 - The puzzle
A campus looks perfect for a marketplace: dense, verified, predictable demand. Facebook launched college by college in 2004. So why do student-built marketplaces die within a year?

## Slide 3 - The network is smaller than the campus
The atomic network on a campus is a residential college, not the whole school. Launch to all of Yale and the first fifty listings are spread across fourteen colleges. Nobody finds anything near them.

## Slide 4 - Everyone multi-homes
Sellers post in GroupMe, the Facebook group and the new app at once. The first answer from anywhere wins. When both sides multi-home, the free channel wins every tie.

## Slide 5 - Testing it
Swapshelf pilot in Branford and Saybrook, Nov 9 to Dec 4. If one college gets liquid, measured as 40% of listings requested within 72 hours, the argument holds. Results in the final paper.
