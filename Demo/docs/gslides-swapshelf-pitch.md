# Swapshelf - founders night pitch

Three minutes at the student founders night, Oct 27.

## Slide 1 - Swapshelf
Swap textbooks and dorm gear without leaving your college.
Theo Park and Sam Okoro, Branford '28

## Slide 2 - $160 in, $15 back
The intro econ textbook costs $160 new. The bookstore buys it back for $15. Two entries down the hall, a first-year buys a new copy.

## Slide 3 - It already happens, badly
About 2,600 books a term already move between Yale students through GroupMe chats and Facebook groups. Posts get buried, people flake, and handoffs take a week to arrange.

## Slide 4 - What we heard
Seven of nine students we interviewed brought up flaking or scheduling before we asked.
"If I could just leave it somewhere and someone takes it, I'd list everything I own."

## Slide 5 - How it works
List in a minute. Drop it on the shelf in your buttery with a pickup code. Earn a credit, spend a credit. Only people in your college can see your stuff.

## Slide 6 - Starting small on purpose
Pilot in Branford and Saybrook from Nov 9. Two colleges, one quadrangle, 40 anchor listers recruited by hand, 200 listings before anyone can request.

## Slide 7 - Traction
212 on the waitlist in two weeks. Landing page conversion went from 4% to 11% when we used students' own words.

## Slide 8 - Ask
Buttery managers in other colleges who'd host a shelf in January. And anyone graduating in May with a mini fridge.
