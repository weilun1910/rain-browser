# PM interview prep hub

Everything for PM interviews in one place. Priya has edit access because we swap mocks.

## Round types
- Product sense: design or improve a product for a user group
- Analytical: metrics, estimation, sometimes a SQL-ish question
- Behavioral: STAR stories, two minutes each
- Design critique (Mapleframe): bring a product you use and say what you'd change

## Product sense structure I actually use
1. Clarify the goal and the constraints
2. Pick one user group and say why
3. Their pains, ranked
4. Three ideas, pick one, say why
5. How you'd know it worked, plus a guardrail

Don't recite a framework out loud. Use it to not get lost.

## Metrics
- Start with what the product is for, then pick the number
- One north star, two or three inputs, one guardrail
- Prioritisation if asked: RICE, MoSCoW, Kano. Know them, don't lead with them.

## Estimation
- Say the assumptions first
- Round hard. 6,500 undergrads, not 6,590.
- Sanity check against something you know
