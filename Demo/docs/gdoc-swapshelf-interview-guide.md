# Swapshelf user interview guide

For the first round, Oct 5 to 11. Aim for 20 to 25 minutes. Don't pitch Swapshelf until the last five minutes, if at all.

## Rules for us
- Ask about the past, not the future. "What did you do last time" beats "would you use".
- No leading questions about price or features.
- Write down their words, not our summary.
- If they say something surprising, ask "tell me more" and shut up.

## Warm up
- Year, college, major
- Where do you live this year?

## Getting rid of things
- Tell me about the last time you got rid of a textbook. What happened?
- What about something bigger, like furniture or a fridge?
- Where did you post it? Did anyone answer?
- What was the annoying part?

## Getting things
- Last time you needed a book for a class, how did you get it?
- Have you ever bought something from another student? How did the handoff work?
- How far would you walk to pick something up?

## Trust
- Have you had someone flake on you? What happened?
- Would it matter whether the other person was in your college?

## Wrap up
- If you could fix one thing about how stuff moves around campus, what would it be?
- Can we follow up when we have something to try?

## After each interview
Put notes in the shared folder within an hour. Tag anyone who could be an anchor lister.
