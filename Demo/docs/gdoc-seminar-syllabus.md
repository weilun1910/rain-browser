# Platforms and Markets - Fall 2026 syllabus

Prof. Ruth Hartley. Wednesdays 1:30 to 3:20. Office hours Thursdays 2 to 4 or by appointment.

## What this seminar is about

How markets work when a company sits in the middle of them. We read the economics of two-sided markets, network effects and platform competition, then look at what happens when regulators try to step in. Most weeks pair one theory paper with one case.

## Weekly schedule

1. What is a platform? Rochet and Tirole, selections
2. Network effects and critical mass
3. Pricing on two sides: who pays and why
4. Multi-homing and competitive bottlenecks
5. Ride-hailing as a case
6. Regulation: the Digital Markets Act and gatekeepers
7. Fall break
8. Data and aggregation
9. Marketplaces: trust, ratings, fraud
10. Labor platforms and the gig economy
11. Lightning talks (5 minutes each)
12. Lightning talks continued
13. Wrap-up

## Assessment

- Weekly response papers, 600 words, 30%
- Discussion, 20%
- Final paper, 5,000 words, 50%

## Final paper

Pick a question about how a real platform or market works and answer it with theory and evidence. Original data is welcome but not required.

- Proposal due Sep 30, one page
- Check-in meeting with me in weeks 6 or 7
- Full draft due Nov 14 for comments
- Lightning talk in week 11 or 12
- Final due Dec 5

## Policies

Late response papers lose a third of a grade per day. Talk to me early if something comes up. AI tools may be used for finding sources, not for writing.
