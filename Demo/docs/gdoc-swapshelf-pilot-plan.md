# Swapshelf pilot launch plan

Branford and Saybrook, Monday Nov 9 to Friday Dec 4. Owner: Theo. Reviewed by Sam.

## Goal

Get one small place to work before we think about the rest of Yale. By the end of the pilot, at least 40% of listings should get a swap request within 72 hours, and completed swaps per week should be going up every week.

We are not trying to get big. Two adjacent colleges, about 900 students, built as one quadrangle. If it doesn't work here, where the walk to a shelf is two minutes and everyone shares a dining hall, it won't work anywhere.

## Why we start by hand

An empty app on day one kills this. Nobody lists until there are people asking, and nobody asks until there's something listed. So for the first week we are the marketplace.

- **Recruit the first listers ourselves.** Sam and I go door to door in every Branford and Saybrook entryway the weekend before launch. We're looking for 40 anchor listers: juniors and seniors with shelves of old textbooks and a mini fridge they're done with. 17 so far.
- **Install it on the spot.** Don't say "we'll send you a link". Take their phone, install the TestFlight build, and list their first three items together right there. A link sent later gets ignored.
- **Seed 200 listings before requests open.** Listers only from Nov 9 to Nov 11. Requests open Thursday Nov 12.
- **Be the delivery.** For the first week we carry items to the buttery shelves ourselves when a lister asks. It doesn't scale. It doesn't need to yet.
- **Thank people by hand.** Every one of the first 40 listers gets a hand-written note and a Blue State coffee card. They should feel like they're part of building this.

## What we're not doing

- No big launch. No posters across campus, no announcement in the YDN, no all-Yale email. A big launch brings a crowd to an empty room.
- No other colleges, even if friends in Pierson ask. Say "January".
- No new features during the pilot unless something is broken.

## Week by week

| Week | Dates | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Nov 6 to 8 | Door to door, anchor listers, TestFlight build 0.4 |
| 1 | Nov 9 to 15 | Listers only until Thursday, then requests open. We carry items. |
| 2 | Nov 16 to 22 | Stop carrying items. Watch the 72-hour request rate. |
| 3 | Nov 23 to 29 | Thanksgiving week, expect a dip. Interview 5 users. |
| 4 | Nov 30 to Dec 4 | Finals rush on gear. Decide on January. |

## What we measure

See the pilot metrics sheet. The short version:
- Completed swaps per week (the one number)
- Share of listings with a request within 72 hours, target 40%
- Missing items from the shelves. If more than 1 in 20 go missing, switch big items to handoffs.

## Risks

- Thanksgiving empties campus for a week right in the middle
- People keep posting in GroupMe and we never see the match. Ask every lister where else they posted.
- Buttery managers change their minds about the shelves

## Decision at the end

Expand to all fourteen colleges in January only if the 72-hour request rate is above 40% for two weeks running. Otherwise, fix whatever breaks and run the pilot again in January in the same two colleges.
