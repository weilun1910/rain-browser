# Swapshelf roadmap

The source of truth. Sam and I both edit this. Last big change Nov 3: pilot moved up a week.

## Now
- Waitlist page live, track signups by college
- Listings: photo, title, condition, kind (textbook or gear), ISBN scan for books
- Swap requests with a 72-hour hold
- Pickup points: shelves in the Branford and Saybrook butteries with a code on a tag
- TestFlight build 0.4 for the pilot
- Door to door for anchor listers, Nov 6 to 8

## Next
- Pilot in Branford and Saybrook, Nov 9 to Dec 4
- Credits: earn one for every item given, spend one per item taken
- Push notification when someone requests your item
- Simple report and block for listings, needed for App Review

## Later
- Open to all fourteen colleges in January for the spring book rush, only if the pilot hits 40% of listings requested within 72 hours
- Wishlist: get pinged when someone lists the book you need
- End-of-year move-out drive in May for furniture
- Maybe a buyback partner so textbooks that don't swap still go somewhere

## Not doing
- Payments. Credits only until we know swaps happen at all.
- Chat. Requests and pickup codes are enough.
- Anything outside Yale.
