# Mapleframe interview prep

Virtual onsite Friday Nov 6. Three 45-minute interviews: product sense, design critique, behavioral. Omar referred me.

## Design critique: GroupMe
Using GroupMe because I complain about it every day, and Omar said to pick something real.
- Who uses it at Yale: whole residential colleges, class years, teams, every club
- What it's great at: zero setup, everyone's already in it, works with SMS
- What breaks: important messages vanish under memes. Selling a textbook in the Branford chat means your post is gone in 20 minutes.
- What I'd change: pinned "asks" that stay at the top until they're resolved, with a reply count
- How I'd know it worked: share of asks that get a reply within an hour, and whether people stop reposting the same ask
