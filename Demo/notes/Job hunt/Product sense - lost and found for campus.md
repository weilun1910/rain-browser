# Product sense - lost and found for campus

Practice prompt from the prep hub: "Design a lost and found product for a university." Timed myself, 20 minutes, talking out loud.

## Who
- Students who lost something. Mostly AirPods, IDs, water bottles, chargers.
- People who found something and want to hand it in with no effort.
- Dining hall and library staff, who end up holding a box of stuff nobody claims.

Picking students who lost something as the focus. They feel the pain, the others just have mild guilt.

## What hurts
- No idea where to look. Every building has its own box.
- By the time you check, it's been a week and it's gone.
- Your ID is the worst one, because you're locked out of everything until it turns up.

## Ideas
1. One shared list across buildings. Staff snap a photo and pick the building.
2. Alerts: say what you lost, get pinged when something similar is logged.
3. ID cards get special handling. Scan the barcode and it notifies the owner directly.

Picking 2. It turns a search into a wait, and you don't have to remember to check.

## Metrics
- Share of logged items returned within 7 days
- Time from logging to pickup
- Guardrail: staff time per item logged. If logging takes more than 30 seconds, staff won't do it.
