# Estimation - textbooks at Yale

Practice for the analytical round. "How many textbooks change hands at Yale each semester?" Picked it because I actually care about the answer for Swapshelf.

## Setup
- About 6,500 undergrads
- Average 4.5 courses a term
- Share of courses that require a physical book you have to get: maybe 40%. Lots of courses use PDFs and course packs.
- Required books per course when there is one: 1.5

## Books needed per term
6,500 x 4.5 x 0.4 x 1.5 = about 17,500 books

## How they get them
- New from the bookstore or online: 40%
- Used, bought online: 30%
- Rented or ebook: 15%
- From another student: 15%

So about 2,600 books a term already change hands between students, through GroupMe, Facebook groups and friends.

## Sanity check
- 2,600 across fourteen colleges is about 190 per college per term
- Most of that happens in the first two weeks of term, so about 13 a day per college at the peak
- Feels right. The Branford GroupMe has a few book posts a day in early September.

## What it means for Swapshelf
- The student-to-student share is the market we can take without changing anyone's behaviour
- If we moved it from 15% to 30% inside one college, that's another 190 books a term there
- Gear (fridges, lamps) isn't in this at all and is probably the steadier half

Next time: say the assumptions out loud first and ask the interviewer if they want a top-down or bottom-up approach.
