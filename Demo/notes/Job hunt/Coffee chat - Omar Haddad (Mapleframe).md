# Coffee chat - Omar Haddad (Mapleframe)

APM at Mapleframe, second year of the APM program. Met him at the CS career fair in September. 25 minutes, coffee at Blue State on York.

## The APM program
- Two years, two rotations. He did the editor team first, now on comments and review.
- Intern class is about twelve people. Most interns who get a return offer go into the APM program.
- Interns ship something real. Last summer one built the version history panel.

## Interview loop
- Recruiter screen
- Product sense: design something for a user group they pick
- Analytical: a metrics question about an existing Mapleframe feature
- Design critique: you bring a product you use and walk through what you'd change. He said this one decides it.
- Behavioral with a director

## His tips
- Pick a design critique product you actually use every day. Interviewers can tell when you prepped a product just for the interview.
- For metrics, start with what the feature is for, then the number. People jump to DAU.
- Mapleframe cares about multiplayer. Cursors, comments, presence. Know why real-time collaboration changes how a team works.

## What I noticed
- He talked more about craft than about growth. Very different vibe from Errandly.
- Comments are their big bet this year. Worth having an opinion on threaded comments vs inline.
