# Quillstone - company notes

Prep for the recruiter screen on Oct 27. Series A, about 40 people, a notes app for people who write a lot. PM intern role on the sync and sharing team.

## What the product is
- Markdown files on your own disk. The app is a view on top of them.
- Local-first. It works offline and syncs when it can. No spinner when you open a note.
- Sync uses CRDTs so two devices can edit the same note offline and merge later without conflicts.
- Links between notes and a backlinks panel. The crowd that likes Zettelkasten and Andy Matuschak's evergreen notes.

## Reading I did
- Ink & Switch, "Local-first software". The seven ideals: fast, multi-device, offline, collaboration, longevity, privacy, user control. Quillstone basically took this essay as a roadmap.
- Matuschak, Evergreen notes: notes should be written to last and to build up across projects, not to file away.
- Wikipedia on CRDTs, enough to not embarrass myself. Two kinds, state-based and operation-based.

## Competitors
- Obsidian: same local files idea, huge plugin ecosystem, sync is a paid add-on
- Notion: collaboration-first, cloud, databases
- Apple Notes: free and good enough for most people

## Why sync and sharing is hard for them
- Local-first sync is solved-ish for one person on two devices
- Sharing a folder with a teammate who isn't on Quillstone is the hard part
- Permissions on plain files are awkward

## What I'd want to ask
- Who's the user they're losing today, and to what?
- How do they think about a shared folder where only one person uses Quillstone?
