# PM internship plan - fall 2026

Goal: a PM internship for summer 2027. Ideally a smaller company where interns ship real things.

## Targets
- About 25 applications total
- Startups and mid-size: Errandly, Mapleframe, Quillstone, plus whatever comes from coffee chats
- A few big tech APM-style programs as a long shot

## Weekly rhythm
- 2 coffee chats
- 3 applications
- 1 mock interview, alternating product sense and behavioral
- Keep the tracker sheet updated every Sunday

## The story
- Swapshelf is the main thing to talk about. A real product with real users, a co-founder, and decisions I can defend.
- The platforms paper backs up that I understand marketplaces, which matters for Errandly especially.
- CS plus econ means I can talk to engineers and do the numbers.

## Deadlines
- Most startup roles open in October and close when they're filled
- Mapleframe: Oct 10
- Errandly: rolling, aim for Oct 20
- Quillstone: rolling

## Things to get better at
- Estimation out loud. I go quiet when I'm doing math.
- Product sense structure without sounding like a template
- Being brief in behavioral answers
