# Behavioral stories

STAR format, but Priya's point from the mock was to spend most of the time on what I did and what changed, not on the setup. Two minutes each, max.

## Conflict: pickup points with Sam
- Situation: Swapshelf, a week before the pilot plan was due. Sam wanted dorm room handoffs, I wanted shelves in the butteries.
- Task: decide without one of us just caving, because we'd both resent it.
- Action: went back to the interview notes. Seven of nine people brought up flakes or scheduling. Proposed shelves with a rule: if more than 1 in 20 items go missing in the first two weeks, we switch to Sam's way.
- Result: Sam agreed because the rule took his worry seriously. Decision in one meeting after a week of going back and forth.
- Priya's note: say what the stakes were. Here it's that a bad handoff experience kills the pilot in week one.
