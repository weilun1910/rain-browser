# Questions to ask PMs

For coffee chats and the end of interviews. Pick three, not ten.

## About their work
- What did you work on last week, concretely?
- What's a decision you made recently that you're not sure was right?
- How do you know when something you shipped worked?
- What does your team argue about?

## About the team
- How do PMs, designers and engineers split who decides what?
- What does a great intern do in the first two weeks?
- What's something the team believed a year ago that turned out to be wrong?

## About getting in
- What did you wish you'd known before your interviews?
- What do strong candidates do that weak ones don't?

## What worked
- "What did you work on last week" got the best answers with Omar. People love explaining their actual week.
- With Nadia, asking how they knew a neighbourhood was ready got 10 minutes of real detail.

## What didn't
- "What's the culture like" gets a brochure answer every time. Stop asking it.
