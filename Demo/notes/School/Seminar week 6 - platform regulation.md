# Seminar week 6 - platform regulation

Platforms and Markets, Wednesday discussion. Readings were on the EU Digital Markets Act and a couple of antitrust cases.

- The DMA names "gatekeepers" by size and user counts and then gives them a list of things they can't do.
- Self-preferencing came up the most. A platform ranking its own products above sellers' products in its own store.
- Interoperability rules: messaging, app stores, default choices.
- Lena argued the rules mostly protect business users, not consumers. Marcus pushed back with app store fees.

Hartley's question for the room: does regulation written for the biggest platforms make it harder for small ones to ever get big? Nobody had a good answer. Compliance costs are fixed, so they hurt small firms more, but none of the rules apply until you're huge.

Response paper due next Wednesday, 600 words. Thinking of writing on default choices, since defaults are their own kind of moat.

Not much here for my final paper. Regulation is about the end state and my paper is about the start.
