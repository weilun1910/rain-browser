# Paper outline

Platforms and Markets final paper. 5,000 words, due Dec 5. Draft to Hartley Nov 14.

**Thesis (v1):** Two-sided marketplaces at universities fail because of turnover and seasonality. A quarter of the users leave every year and demand only shows up twice a semester.

## 1. Intro
- Campus marketplaces everyone has seen: textbook exchanges, sublets, free-and-for-sale Facebook groups, GroupMe chats
- Most student-built ones die within a year. Why?

## 2. Theory
- Network effects, direct and cross-side
- Two-sided markets: Rochet and Tirole 2003, price structure over price level
- Critical mass and the chicken-or-egg problem

## 3. What's different about a campus
- Turnover: every class graduates
- Seasonality: textbooks in late August and January, furniture in May
- Density: everyone in walking distance, one email domain

## 4. Cases
- Facebook at Harvard in 2004 (worked)
- A textbook exchange that didn't (find one with real numbers)
- Maybe Tinder at USC

## 5. What would work
- Launch around the seasonal spikes
- Hand off between graduating and incoming students
