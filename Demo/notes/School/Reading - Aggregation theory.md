# Reading - Aggregation theory

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, 2015.

His claim is that the internet moved power from whoever controls supply to whoever controls demand. Aggregators own the relationship with users, have close to zero marginal cost to serve one more user, and suppliers line up to be on them because that's where the users are. Google for websites, Facebook for publishers, Airbnb for rooms.

Why it's only half useful for the paper:
- Aggregation explains what happens after a platform wins. My question is about the very beginning, when there's no demand to control yet.
- But it does explain why the free channels are so sticky. A class GroupMe already owns the demand for "anyone selling ECON 117 books". A new app has to pull users off something that already aggregates them, for free.

One line to keep: suppliers come to the aggregator because that's where the users are. The reverse is the whole problem at launch. On day one, nobody is there.

Probably one paragraph in section 3, not more.
