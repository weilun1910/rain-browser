# Multi-homing evidence

Hartley's ask from the Oct 17 check-in: back up the multi-homing claim with something better than "everyone uses GroupMe".

## What I have
- **Chen, Chevalier, Rossi and Oehlsen, "The Value of Flexible Work: Evidence from Uber Drivers" (NBER w23296).** About how much drivers value choosing their own hours. It's adjacent. Drivers who can switch on and off at will can also switch between apps, but the paper doesn't measure that directly.
- **Cohen et al., "Using Big Data to Estimate Consumer Surplus: The Case of Uber" (NBER w22627).** Demand side, surge pricing. Not about multi-homing. Probably cut.
- **Eisenmann, Parker and Van Alstyne, "Strategies for Two-Sided Markets", HBR 2006.** Talks about multi-homing costs and when a platform should fight for exclusivity. Good framing source.
- **Wikipedia, two-sided market.** Has a short section on multi-homing that points at the theory papers.

Careful: Wikipedia's "Multihoming" article is about connecting a computer to more than one network. Wrong field entirely. Don't cite it.
