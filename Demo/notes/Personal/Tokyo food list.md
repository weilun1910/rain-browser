# Tokyo food list

## Ramen
- **Fuunji, Shinjuku.** Tsukemen, the thick dipping noodles. Recommended by Nadia from the Errandly coffee chat, see [[Coffee chat - Nadia Brooks (Errandly)]]. Go before 11am or expect an hour in line. Buy a ticket from the machine first.
- Afuri, yuzu shio ramen. Lighter, a few locations, good if the line at Fuunji is crazy.
- Ichiran. Solo booths. Touristy but Ben wants to do it once.

## Breakfast and markets
- Tsukiji outer market. The inner market moved to Toyosu in 2018, but the outer market is still there. Tamagoyaki and fresh sushi in the morning.
- Konbini breakfast at least once. The egg sandwich, everyone says so.

## Other
- Depachika food halls in department store basements, for picnic stuff before the Hakone train
- Izakaya in Omoide Yokocho near Shinjuku station. Tiny, smoky, go early
- Yakitori somewhere under the train tracks in Yurakucho

## Kyoto, so I don't forget
- Nishiki Market for lunch one day
- Tofu in Arashiyama after the bamboo grove

Priya is vegetarian most of the time. Afuri has a vegan option. Check the others.
