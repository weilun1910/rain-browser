# Japanese phrases to learn

Doing ten minutes a day in Anki before the trip. Hiragana first, so menus and station signs aren't pure noise.

## Hiragana
- 46 basic characters. Done with the first 25 as of Nov 2.
- Spaced repetition actually works for this. Cards I miss come back the same day, cards I know come back in a week.

## Phrases
- Sumimasen: excuse me, sorry, and getting a waiter's attention
- Arigatou gozaimasu: thank you, the polite one
- Kore o kudasai: this one, please (point at the menu)
- Ikura desu ka: how much is it?
- Eki wa doko desu ka: where's the station?
- Eigo no menyuu wa arimasu ka: is there an English menu?
- Bejitarian: Priya will need this one, plus "niku nashi" for no meat
- Oishii: delicious. Use it a lot.

## Things not to do
- Don't tip. It can come across as rude.
- Don't eat while walking in most places
- Stand on the left on escalators in Tokyo. Osaka does it on the right, apparently.
