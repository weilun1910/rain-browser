# JR Pass math

Is the 7-day JR Pass worth it for our route? Short answer: no.

## The pass
- The price went up a lot in October 2023. The 7-day ordinary pass is now ¥50,000.
- You can ride the Nozomi now if you pay an extra fee on top. Before, the pass didn't cover it at all.

## Our long train trips, one way, rough prices
- Tokyo to Odawara (for Hakone): about ¥3,300 on the Shinkansen
- Odawara to Kyoto: about ¥11,000
- Kyoto to Nara and back: about ¥1,500 total on the JR Nara line
- Kyoto to Tokyo: about ¥14,000

Total: about ¥30,000. That's ¥20,000 less than the pass.

## Other stuff
- The Hakone loop (the switchback train, the ropeway, the boat) is private lines. The Hakone Free Pass covers those, not the JR Pass.
- In Tokyo and Kyoto we're mostly on the metro and buses, so the JR Pass doesn't help there either.
- Suica on the iPhone covers all the local stuff. Add it to Wallet before the flight.

## Decision
Skip the JR Pass. Buy Shinkansen tickets as we go or the day before. Get the Hakone Free Pass from Odawara.
