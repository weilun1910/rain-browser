# Kyoto shortlist

Four days. January is quiet season, but the big sights are still busy by mid-morning. Everything good happens before 9am.

## Must
- Fushimi Inari. Go at sunrise and hike up past where the crowds turn around. The full loop takes about two hours.
- Kiyomizu-dera, then walk down through Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka
- Arashiyama bamboo grove at 7am, then Tenryu-ji when it opens
- Kinkaku-ji. Touristy, but it's the gold one and Priya wants the photo

## If there's time
- Philosopher's Walk, from Ginkaku-ji down to Nanzen-ji. Better in spring but still nice.
- Gion in the evening. Don't follow anyone around for photos.
- Nishiki Market for lunch

## Day trip to Nara
- Nara Park and the deer. Buy the crackers, bow to the deer, they bow back.
- Todai-ji and the giant Buddha
- Train from Kyoto is under an hour

## Rough plan
- Day 1: Fushimi Inari early, Kiyomizu-dera in the afternoon
- Day 2: Arashiyama early, Kinkaku-ji
- Day 3: Nara
- Day 4: Philosopher's Walk, Nishiki, slow day, Gion at night
