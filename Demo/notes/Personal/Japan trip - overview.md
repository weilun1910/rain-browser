# Japan trip - overview

Jan 3 to Jan 14, 2027. Me, Ben and Priya. First time in Japan for all three of us. Ben's doc has the day by day itinerary, this is my own overview.

## Route
- Tokyo, 4 nights (Shinjuku)
- Hakone, 1 night in a ryokan with an onsen
- Kyoto, 4 nights, with a day trip to Nara
- Tokyo, 2 nights, then fly home

## Decided
- Hotel in Shinjuku for the first leg, near the station
- One ryokan night in Hakone, kaiseki dinner included. It's the splurge.
- Nara as a day trip from Kyoto, not an overnight
