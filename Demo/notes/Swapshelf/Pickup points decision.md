# Pickup points decision

Decided Oct 20 at the weekly sync. Writing down why, because Sam and I went back and forth for a week.

## Options
1. Dorm room handoff. Requester comes to the lister's room.
2. Buttery shelves. A shelf in each college's buttery, item tagged with a four-digit code, requester takes it.
3. Dining hall table at fixed hours, twice a week.

## What we picked
Buttery shelves in Branford and Saybrook, with option 1 as a fallback for big gear like fridges and chairs.

## Why
- Interviews: seven of nine people brought up flakes or scheduling. Shelves remove both.
- Aisha doesn't want strangers at her door, and she's exactly the anchor lister we need.
- Butteries are open late, and that's when people actually do this.
- The dining hall table needs one of us sitting there. Doesn't survive a midterm week.

## Sam's case for dorm handoffs
He thought shelves would get messy and things would go missing, and that meeting in person builds trust. Fair. We agreed to track missing items in the first two weeks and switch back if more than 1 in 20 goes missing.

## What we need
- OK from both buttery managers (Sam asked Branford, I'm asking Saybrook)
- Printed tags with codes
- A "picked up" button so the lister knows it's gone
