# User interviews - synthesis

Nine interviews, Oct 5 to Oct 11. Five Branford, three Saybrook, one Pierson as a control. Notes in [[User interview - Aisha (Branford)]], [[User interview - Daniel (Saybrook)]] and the doc with the rest.

## What kept coming up
1. The handoff is the expensive part. Seven of nine brought up flakes or scheduling before we asked.
2. Everyone already posts in two or three places. GroupMe, the Facebook group, sometimes Marketplace. Nobody is loyal to any of them.
3. College identity matters more than Yale identity. People trust "someone in Branford" more than "someone at Yale".
4. Textbook timing is spiky. The first two weeks of term and finals week. Gear is steadier than we assumed.
5. Sellers want to get rid of things fast. Buyers want to browse ahead of time.

## What it means for the product
- Pickup points instead of meetups. A shelf with a code beats coordinating a time.
- Launch inside one or two colleges, not all of Yale, so there's always something nearby.
- Seed listings before inviting buyers. An empty app on day one would kill it.
- Don't fight GroupMe. Let people share a listing link into it.

## Open questions
- Is the Branford and Saybrook pair big enough to feel alive, or do we need a third college?
- Will juniors and seniors list during the term, or only at move-out?

Quote to keep: "If I could just leave it somewhere and someone takes it, I'd list everything I own." (Aisha)
