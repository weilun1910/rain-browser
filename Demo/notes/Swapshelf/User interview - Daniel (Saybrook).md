# User interview - Daniel (Saybrook)

First-year in Saybrook, lives on Old Campus like most first-years. Undecided, probably CS. 20 minutes after his section.

Context:
- Needed a mini fridge in August. Bought one on Amazon for $160 because the Facebook group had one listing and it was gone.
- Has four textbooks this term. Bought two used online, two new.

What he'd use:
- Wants to see what's available before he needs it, not post a request and wait
- Would walk to his college but not across campus. "Old Campus to Saybrook is fine. Old Campus to Science Hill is not happening."
- Likes the idea of credits. "I don't want to Venmo a stranger $8 for a lamp."

Trust:
- Yale email is enough for him
- Would want to see that the person is in his college

Surprise:
- He'd pay credits for a mini fridge right now, in October. Gear demand isn't only in August.

Takeaways:
- Buyers want to browse, sellers want to dump. Design for both.
- First-years are buyers. Juniors and seniors are sellers. Mixing years inside one college is the whole trick.
