# Swapshelf pilot metrics

What we watch during the Branford and Saybrook pilot, Nov 9 to Dec 4. Agreed with Sam at the Oct 29 sync.

## The one number
Completed swaps per week in the two pilot colleges. If this isn't going up by week 2, nothing else matters.

## Liquidity
- Share of listings that get a swap request within 72 hours. Target 40%.
- Time from a new user's first open to their first completed swap.
- Listings per active lister. Are the anchor listers carrying everything?

## Supply health
- Anchor listers recruited by hand before launch: goal 40
- Listings seeded before requests open: goal 200
- Share of listings that are textbooks vs gear

## Funnel
- Waitlist to installed the TestFlight build
- Installed to listed something
- Installed to requested something

## Where we are (Oct 29)
- Waitlist: 212 signups. Branford 88, Saybrook 64, everyone else 60.
- Landing page converted at 4% the first week and 11% after we changed the headline to "Swap textbooks without leaving your college".
- Anchor listers so far: 17.

## Who wants these numbers
- Hartley wants them for the paper case study by Nov 10. Send the waitlist split and whatever pilot data exists by then.
- From the mock interview, Priya said I should be able to name the one number and how I'd move it in under a minute. That's the swaps per week line above.
