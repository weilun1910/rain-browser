# User interview - Aisha (Branford)

Sophomore, Branford, MCDB major. 25 minutes in the Branford dining hall. Used the interview guide, tried not to pitch.

Last time she got rid of a textbook:
- Bought the orgo book new for $140 last fall. Sold it back to the bookstore for $12 in May.
- "I knew someone in my college probably needed it. I just didn't know who and I wasn't going to chase it."

What she does now:
- Posts in the Branford GroupMe when she has something. Gets a reply maybe half the time.
- Checks the Facebook free-and-for-sale group "when I'm bored, not when I need something."

Pain:
- Flakes. Two people said they'd pick up a lamp and never came.
- Coordinating a time is the annoying part, not finding a buyer.
- Doesn't want strangers coming to her room.

What she'd want:
- "If I could just leave it somewhere and someone takes it, I'd list everything I own."
- Would trust it more if it was only Branford people.

Takeaways:
- The handoff is the cost, not the listing
- A drop-off spot would beat any feature
- She's an anchor lister type: lots of stuff, moving out of a double next year
