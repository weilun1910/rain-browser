# Engine | round 3 plan

Written 2026-11-05 by the Engine agent. Research is in [2026-11-04-on-device-retrieval.md](../research/2026-11-04-on-device-retrieval.md) and [2026-11-04-sources.md](../research/2026-11-04-sources.md).

## What I learned

- llama.cpp's official xcframework works as a SwiftPM binary target with plain `swift build`, with Metal and no shader step. I tested it on Felix's Mac with real models.
- EmbeddingGemma 2 (Apache 2.0, 310 MB) and Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B (Apache 2.0, 396 MB) both run through it. Embedding runs at about 10,000 tokens a second, and reranking 20 passages takes about 1.2 s.
- Foundation Models on the 26.2 SDK has no embeddings. Guided generation works for describing what Felix is doing (2.7 s) and for a yes or no judgment (about 1 s per item). The on-device model is too literal to be the only gate, so it backs up the reranker instead of replacing it.
- `NLContextualEmbedding` is on the Mac with no download and is the fallback.
- Granola's local files are encrypted and closed to other apps. Its official MCP server with OAuth is the route. Notion's desktop cache is plain SQLite with about 3,500 pages on Felix's Mac. Google Docs text comes from the export URLs with the browser's own session.

## What gets built

### 1. Models on device
- `Package.swift` gets a `llama` binary target (b12121 xcframework) and the app target depends on it.
- `scripts/build.sh` copies `llama.framework` into the app's `Contents/Frameworks` and adds the rpath.
- `Intelligence/ModelStore.swift` downloads the two GGUF files into `Paths.root/Models/`, so `--data-dir` keeps them separate. It resumes with HTTP Range requests, checks size and sha256, and reports progress. Downloads start only when Felix clicks Download in Settings, and they pause when he quits.
- `Intelligence/Embeddings.swift` picks the best embedder that's ready: EmbeddingGemma 2, then `NLContextualEmbedding`, then the old sentence embedding. Every stored vector records which model made it.
- A background job re-embeds passages whose vectors came from an older model, in small batches at utility priority, so search gets better on its own once the download finishes.

### 2. Passages and vectors
- Passages stay as they are (about 700 characters along paragraph and sentence lines). Each one is embedded with the document prompt `title: <title> | text: <passage>`.
- Vectors live in one contiguous matrix in memory and are scanned with Accelerate. No vector database until measurements say so.

### 3. Working context
- `Intelligence/WorkingContext.swift` asks Foundation Models, with guided generation, for a `WorkingContext`: topic, intent, entities and three search queries. The input is a trimmed window of what's in front of Felix: the title, the start of the visible text, and for notes the last part he typed.
- It runs after the context settles (1.5 s debounce) and only when the text changed meaningfully (word overlap under 85% with the last run). Results are cached per item.
- Without Foundation Models it falls back to the title, the top keywords and `NLTagger` names.

### 4. Hybrid retrieval and judging
- `Index/Retrieval.swift` runs four retrievers over passages: FTS5 BM25 on the context's words, BM25 on the model's queries, dense search with the query vectors, and entity matches. Reciprocal rank fusion (k = 60) merges them into one candidate list per section.
- The top 24 candidates go to the Qwen3 reranker, with the working context as the query and the best passage of each item as the document.
- The top few that clear the reranker bar go to Foundation Models for a structured judgment: helpful yes or no, a confidence, and a one-sentence reason that only uses facts from the item. A confident no removes a borderline card. A strong reranker score can't be vetoed by a weak no.
- Without the reranker, the fused score and the old keyword and meaning checks gate the cards, so the panel still works during the download.
- The current item, anything opened in the last 10 minutes, and dismissed items are excluded as before. A Notion or Google Doc read in the browser and the same document from its source count as one item.

### 5. Stable results
- Cards that are on screen stay unless their score falls below 75% of the bar (hysteresis). New cards append below. Cards don't reshuffle while Felix types.
- The context only re-runs when it changed meaningfully, and the old cards stay up until the new run finishes.

### 6. Reasons grounded in history
- A new `visits` table records each capture with what Felix was doing then: the note or doc he was writing in the last 20 minutes, or the space he was in.
- The reason line says when and in what context he saw it, for example "You read this on 12 Oct while writing Thesis outline." The judgment's reason sentence follows it. No vague lines like "close in topic".

### 7. Entities and topics per item
- Entities come from `NLTagger` at index time, as now. Topics come from Foundation Models' content tagging model in a background job, with keyword topics as the fallback. Both are stored in `entities` and a new `topics` table.

### 8. New sources
Each has a switch in Settings that says what it reads. All are read-only.
- **Notion** (`Sources/NotionSource.swift`): copies `notion.db` with its WAL into the app's tmp folder, reads the copy, rebuilds each live page's text from its blocks and deletes the copy. A macOS permission denial shows the same help as the Chrome importer. `--notion-dry-run` prints counts and field names only.
- **Google Docs** (`Sources/GoogleDocsSource.swift`): takes every Docs, Sheets and Slides URL from this browser's history and the Chrome import, then fetches each text export with the app's own WebKit cookies, three at a time, at most once a day per document. A redirect to the sign-in page stops the run and says so.
- **Granola** (`Sources/GranolaSource.swift`, `Intelligence/MCPClient.swift`, `Intelligence/OAuth.swift`): Connect opens Granola's sign-in in the default browser. The app registers itself through OAuth dynamic registration, uses PKCE and catches the redirect on 127.0.0.1. It then syncs meetings through `list_meetings` and `get_meetings`. `--granola-dry-run` reports which local files exist and that they're encrypted, and fetches the public OAuth metadata.

### 9. Settings
- Sources gets the three new switches and their status lines, next to Chrome and Notes folders.
- A new Models section shows each model, its size, download progress, and which embedder and reranker are in use. Tuning keeps the threshold slider, which now scales the reranker bar, and drops the three weight sliders, which no longer mean anything.

### 10. Measuring
- `Tests/EngineEval.swift` holds a labelled fixture: about 40 items across several topics, and 14 contexts, each with the items that should surface and some that look close but shouldn't. Four contexts should show nothing.
- `--eval` runs the old pipeline and the new one on the same throwaway index and prints precision at 3 for each, plus how many should-be-empty contexts stayed empty.

## Interfaces for Graph and MCP

These live in `Index/IndexStore+API.swift`. All are synchronous and thread-safe. Call them off the main thread.

```swift
struct Neighbour { let doc: DocMeta; let similarity: Double; let passage: String }
struct ItemEntity { let name: String; let display: String; let kind: String; let count: Int }
struct SearchFilters {
    var sources: Set<SourceType>? = nil   // nil means every source
    var section: CardSection? = nil       // sites or docs
    var since: Date? = nil                // last seen or edited on or after
    var until: Date? = nil
    var rerank: Bool = true               // use the cross-encoder when it's ready
}
struct SearchHit { let doc: DocMeta; let score: Double; let passage: String; let lastSeen: Date; let history: String }

extension IndexStore {
    func item(id: Int64) -> DocMeta?
    func semanticNeighbours(itemID: Int64, k: Int = 10) -> [Neighbour]
    func entities(itemID: Int64) -> [ItemEntity]
    func topics(itemID: Int64) -> [String]
    func search(_ query: String, filters: SearchFilters = SearchFilters(), limit: Int = 20) -> [SearchHit]
}
```

- `semanticNeighbours` averages the item's passage vectors, scans every other item, and returns the top k by cosine similarity, with the closest passage.
- `search` runs the same hybrid retrieval as Related (BM25, dense and entities with rank fusion, then the reranker when it's ready), without the Foundation Models judgment, so it stays fast.
- `SourceType` gains `notion`, `gdocs` and `granola`. Graph should treat unknown source names as their own kind, which its plan already does.

### Measured
- Eval, precision at 3 (share of shown cards that were labelled useful, up to 3 per section), recall at 3, and quiet contexts that stayed empty:

| Pipeline | Precision at 3 | Recall at 3 | Quiet | Time per context |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round 2 (sentence vectors, weighted score) | 0.94 | 0.71 | 4 of 5 | 14 ms |
| Round 3, built-in embedding, no reranker | 0.93 | 0.67 | 4 of 5 | 15 ms |
| Round 3, built-in embedding and Foundation Models | 0.93 to 1.00 | 0.67 | 4 of 5 | 2.0 s |
| Round 3, EmbeddingGemma 2, no reranker | 0.83 | 0.86 | 4 of 5 | 16 ms |
| Round 3, EmbeddingGemma 2 and reranker | 1.00 | 0.86 | 5 of 5 | 1.0 s |
| Round 3, full (adds Foundation Models context and judgment) | 1.00 | 0.86 | 5 of 5 | 2.7 s |

- The fixture is small and I wrote it, so these numbers show direction, not product quality. The Demo agent's labelled set should replace it.
- Foundation Models isn't fully deterministic even at temperature 0, so the rows that use it move by a card or two between runs.
- The reranker's scores sit near 0 or 1: relevant items scored 0.55 to 1.00 and unrelated ones under 0.1. That's why its bar sits at 0.15 by default and the judgment handles the band up to 0.85.
- Retrieval over 20,000 synthetic passages takes about 110 ms and over 60,000 about 370 ms, with a flat scan. Felix's index is under 20,000 passages, so there's no vector database. A contiguous matrix with BLAS is the next step past about 50,000.
- Download, stop and resume of both models from Hugging Face worked end to end, with checksums verified.
- Notion dry run on Felix's Mac: 3,528 live pages, 1,536 with text that would be indexed. Granola dry run: every data file is encrypted, and Granola's public sign-in metadata reads fine.
